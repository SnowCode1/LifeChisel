Four kinds of habit

Some habits are done or not done. Others are better as a number, a choice or a few words. All four sit in the same grid.

Checkbox

Done or not. One tap ticks it.

Goal options

A ticked day counts as done. You can flip that for things you're cutting out, so an unticked day counts, or start every day ticked and untick the days you slip.

Number

Minutes, glasses, pages, kilometres. Shaded when you reach your target.

Goal options

At least an amount, at most an amount, or between two. When you enter a number, your recent amounts are offered so you rarely need to type.

Goals you set yourself

Each habit can have its own goal, or none at all if you only want to keep a record.

If you change a goal later, you choose the date it starts from. Days before that are still measured against the old goal, so your past results stay the same.

All the ways a goal can repeat
  • Every day, or only on the weekdays you pick
  • A number of days per week: at least, at most, or between two numbers
  • The same over a period of your own length, from 1 to 366 days
  • On and off cycles: 5 days on and 2 off, 3 weeks on and 1 off, alternate weeks, or your own pattern

History and Undo

History lists the changes you've made, day by day. If you tick the wrong box or archive a habit by accident, Undo puts it back.

Everything can also go into a single backup file, which you can open again on any device.

What History keeps
  • Every entry, including changes to earlier days
  • Edits to a habit: its name, colour, notes and goal
  • Archiving, restoring and deleting habits, and changes to their order
  • A calendar of the days you made changes, and filters by habit

What counts toward the five habits?

Only active habits. Archive one you've finished with and it no longer counts, with all its records kept.

What happens to my records if I don't upgrade?

Nothing. The limit only stops you adding a sixth habit. Your existing habits, History, exports and backups keep working either way.

Where are my habits stored?

On your device. If you sign in and turn on sync, they're also kept up to date on your other devices. You can export a backup file at any time.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and Android, plus any computer through the web app.
