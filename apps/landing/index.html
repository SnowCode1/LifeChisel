Built to stay out of your way

  • One grid, every habit

    Habits down the side, days across the top, in the order you choose. Rows never jump around, so your hand learns where to tap.

  • More than checkboxes

    Tick a box, count minutes or glasses, pick a category like Run or Gym, or jot a line of text, all in the same grid.

  • Goals that fit real weeks

    Every day, chosen weekdays, or three times a week. Change a goal from a date onwards and earlier results stay as they were.

  • Statistics when you want them

    Streaks, trends, a calendar, the days and times you do best. They are one tap away and never in the way of tracking.

  • Nothing is ever lost

    Every change is recorded in History and can be undone. Export a full backup whenever you like and open it on another device.

  • No account needed

    Your habits are saved on your device. The source is open under the GPL, so anyone can check how it works.
