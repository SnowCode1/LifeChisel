The grid

See your whole week at a glance.

Today is on the right and the days before it run to the left. Boxes joined by a bar are a streak. A cross is a day you missed.

You can tell how the week went before you've read a word.

Goals

Set a goal that suits each habit.

Three times a week. Weekdays only. Five days on, two off. No more than two coffees. Or no goal, if you only want a record.

When you change a goal, you choose the day it starts. The weeks before keep the results they had.

All the ways a goal can repeat
  • Every day, or only on the weekdays you pick
  • A number of days per week: at least, at most, or between two numbers
  • The same over a period of your own length, from 1 to 366 days
  • On and off cycles: 5 days on and 2 off, 3 weeks on and 1 off, alternate weeks, or your own pattern

History

Mistakes take one tap to fix.

Ticked the wrong day, or archived a habit by accident? Undo puts it back. History lists every change you've made, day by day.

Everything can go into one backup file, which opens again on any device.

What History keeps
  • Every entry, including changes to earlier days
  • Edits to a habit: its name, colour, notes and goal
  • Archiving, restoring and deleting habits, and changes to their order
  • A calendar of the days you made changes, and filters by habit

What counts toward the five habits?

Only habits you're using. Archive one you've finished with and it stops counting, with all its records kept.

What happens if I stay on the free version?

Everything keeps working. The limit only stops you adding a sixth habit.

Where are my habits stored?

On your device. If you sign in and turn on sync, they're also kept up to date on your other devices. You can export a backup file at any time.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone and Android, plus any computer through the browser.
